WEEK 1: A change you got used to
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rule: past-simple-finished
  ruleExamples:
    - I moved to Madrid in 2022.
    - We visited Paris two years ago.
  Mapped items (4):
    * W1-C1 [Choose] I ___ to Bristol three years ago.
      key   : moved
      reason: "Three years ago" is a finished time.
    * W1-C6 [Choose] She ___ her job last month and is now looking for a new one.
      key   : left
      reason: "Last month" is a finished time.
    * W1-G4 [Complete] Last summer I ___ (spend) two weeks in Scotland.
      key   : spent
      reason: Finished time.
    * W1-F1 [Fix] I have moved to Leeds in 2023.
      key   : I moved to Leeds in 2023.
      reason: "In 2023" is a finished time, so use the past simple.

Rule: present-perfect-experience
  ruleExamples:
    - I've lived here since 2021.
    - Have you ever worked abroad?
  Mapped items (4):
    * W1-C2 [Choose] I ___ a lot of new friends since then.
      key   : have made
      reason: "Since then" means up to now.
    * W1-C5 [Choose] ___ you ever ___ in another country?
      key   : Have ... lived | accepted:  / 
      reason: "Ever" asks about life experience up to now.
    * W1-G3 [Complete] I ___ (know) Maria since we were at school.
      key   : have known
      reason: Up to now with "since"; know is not used in the continuous.
    * W1-E3 [Extra] She ___ (work) here since 2019.
      key   : has worked | accepted: has been working
      reason: Up to now with "since"; both forms are correct.

Rule: past-perfect
  ruleExamples:
    - When I arrived, the train had already left.
    - She was tired because she hadn't slept well.
  Mapped items (7):
    * W1-C3 [Choose] When I arrived at the station, the train ___.
      key   : had left
      reason: It left before I arrived.
    * W1-G2 [Complete] By the time I got to the party, most people ___ (go) home.
      key   : had gone
      reason: They left before I arrived.
    * W1-G5 [Complete] She was tired because she ___ (not sleep) the night before.
      key   : hadn't slept | accepted: had not slept
      reason: Earlier than the moment she was tired.
    * W1-F2 [Fix] When I got home, my flatmate already cooked dinner.
      key   : When I got home, my flatmate had already cooked dinner.
      reason: Cooking happened before I got home.
    * W1-F3 [Fix] I was knowing her for years before we worked together.
      key   : I had known her for years before we worked together. | accepted: I knew her for years before we worked together.
      reason: Know is a state verb, and this happened before the other past event.
    * W1-R2 [Rewrite] First the film started. Then we arrived. (When ... already)
      key   : When we arrived, the film had already started. | accepted: The film had already started when we arrived.
      reason: The past perfect shows which happened first.
    * W1-E2 [Extra] I ___ (never / see) snow until I moved to Canada.
      key   : had never seen
      reason: Before another past event.

Rule: past-continuous
  ruleExamples:
    - While we were walking home, it started to rain.
    - I was cooking dinner when my phone rang.
  Mapped items (4):
    * W1-C4 [Choose] I ___ dinner when my phone rang.
      key   : was cooking
      reason: A longer action interrupted by a short one.
    * W1-G1 [Complete] While we ___ (drive) to the coast, it started to rain.
      key   : were driving
      reason: Background action.
    * W1-R3 [Rewrite] I was walking home. I saw an accident. (While)
      key   : While I was walking home, I saw an accident. | accepted: I saw an accident while I was walking home.
      reason: Background plus event.
    * W1-E1 [Extra] We ___ (watch) TV when the lights went out.
      key   : were watching
      reason: Background action interrupted.

Rule: used-to-would
  ruleExamples:
    - We used to live in a small flat.
    - Every summer, my grandfather would take us fishing.
  Mapped items (2):
    * W1-L1 [Light] When I was a child, we ___ in a small flat.
      key   : used to live | accepted: lived
      reason: Live is a state, so would is not possible.
    * W1-L2 [Light] Every summer, my grandfather ___ us fishing.
      key   : would take | accepted: used to take / took
      reason: A repeated action in the past.

Rule: present-perfect-continuous
  ruleExamples:
    - I've been studying English for three years.
    - She's tired because she's been working all morning.
  Mapped items (2):
    * W1-R1 [Rewrite] I started learning English in 2020. I'm still learning it. (since)
      key   : I've been learning English since 2020. | accepted: I have been learning English since 2020.
      reason: An activity that continues up to now.
    * W1-L3 [Light] I'm tired because I ___ all morning.
      key   : 've been studying
      reason: An activity up to now with a result you can see.

Rule: present-simple-habit

     es          : (omitted — transparent)

  3. challenge (n., B1)
     collocation : face a challenge
     es          : (omitted — transparent)

     extraExample: Everyone has to face a challenge when they start a new job.

     collocation : get used to
     es          : acostumbrarse a + infinitivo = get used to + -ing (not get used to live)
