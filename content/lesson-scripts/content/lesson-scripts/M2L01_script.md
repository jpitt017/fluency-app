# Module 2, Lesson 1: Vowel Sounds: /iː/ vs /ɪ/

## Overview
* **Duration:** 12 minutes
* **Objective:** Master the distinction between the long tense /iː/ sound (as in *sheep*) and the short lax /ɪ/ sound (as in *ship*).
* **Target Audio / Vault Items:** W001 through W005 (Shadowing & Minimal Pairs).

---

## Script & Dialogue

### Scene 1: Minimal Pairs Drill
**Instructor:** "Listen carefully and repeat after me. Pay attention to tongue tension and jaw position."

* **Pair 1:** "Sheep" (/iː/) vs "Ship" (/ɪ/)
* **Pair 2:** "Beat" (/iː/) vs "Bit" (/ɪ/)

### Scene 2: High-Frequency Focus Words
**Instructor:** "These vowel contrasts appear constantly in natural spoken English. Let's practice words like **thought**, **business**, **comfortable**, **interesting**, and **probably**."

---

## Practice Notes
* `/iː/`: Tense, lips spread wide, tongue high near the roof of the mouth.
* `/ɪ/`: Lax, lips relaxed, tongue slightly lower and further back.
