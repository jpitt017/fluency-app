# Module 1, Lesson 1: Phrasal Verbs for Daily Routines

## Overview
* **Duration:** 12 minutes
* **Objective:** Master 4 high-frequency phrasal verbs used in daily routines (Wake up, Get ready, Set off, Wind down).
* **Target Audio / Vault Items:** Daily routine transitions.

---

## Script & Dialogue

### Scene 1: Morning Routine
**Speaker A:** "I usually **wake up** around 6:30 AM, but I rarely get out of bed immediately."  
**Speaker B:** "Me too. It takes me twenty minutes just to **get ready** for work."

### Scene 2: Commuting & Evening
**Speaker A:** "What time do you usually **set off** in the morning?"  
**Speaker B:** "Around 7:45 AM to beat the traffic. And by the time I get home, I just want to **wind down** with a book."

---

## Key Vocabulary Breakdown
1. **Wake up** – To stop sleeping.
2. **Get ready** – To prepare oneself (for an activity, work, etc.).
3. **Set off** – To start a journey.
4. **Wind down** – To relax after a period of work or tension.
